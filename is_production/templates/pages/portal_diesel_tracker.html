How it is worked out

Everything comes from the production records, every hour: diesel from the submitted Daily Diesel Sheets; the tallies from Hourly Production, the truck loads each excavator filled and each truck hauled, and production dozing. A truck's loads are its excavator's bcm, so the site counts them once.

The site's volume is the actual volume, as in the Monthly Production Planning: the tallies with the surveys applied. Within a production month, the last survey's variance falls on the days up to the survey, in proportion to their tallies, and the days after it count as tallied; so a whole production month comes to the plan's Monthly Actual BCMs. Coal: the surveyed coal tons up to the survey, then the tallied coal × 1.5 t/bcm.

Overall L/BCM = the site's diesel litres ÷ its actual bcm. A machine's L/BCM = its diesel ÷ its own tallied bcm (the surveys are by site, not by machine). Machines that moved no volume (water carts, graders, light vehicles) show their diesel but no L/BCM.

Burn rate = diesel litres ÷ engine hours, for each ADT, excavator and dozer and for each fleet. Engine hours come from the Pre-Use Hours sheets: the night shift's closing hour-meter reading less the day shift's opening one, as in the Monthly Diesel Reconciliation. The OEM burn rate is the model's (OEM Diesel Burn Rate) at the end of the period.
