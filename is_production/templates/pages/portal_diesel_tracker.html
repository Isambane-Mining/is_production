How L/BCM is worked out

Total bcm = waste bcm + coal tons ÷ coal density.

L/BCM = diesel litres ÷ total bcm, for each machine and for the whole site.

From the production records: diesel from the submitted Daily Diesel Sheets; volumes from Hourly Production, the truck loads each excavator filled and each truck hauled, and production dozing. A truck's loads are its excavator's bcm, so they count once in the site's total.

Burn rate = diesel litres ÷ engine hours, for each ADT, excavator and dozer and for each fleet. Engine hours come from the Pre-Use Hours sheets: the night shift's closing hour-meter reading less the day shift's opening one, as in the Monthly Diesel Reconciliation. The OEM burn rate is the model's (OEM Diesel Burn Rate) at the end of the period.

Current coal density: 1.4 t/bcm. Machines that moved no volume (water carts, graders, light vehicles) show their diesel but no L/BCM. Change density and the gauge bands under Setup.
